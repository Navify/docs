Privacy Policy

Navify customizes Spotify Web locally in your browser.

Effective July 31, 2026.

Navify does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data handling

The extension stores feature preferences and installed Marketplace customizations using browser extension storage. Synced preferences may be synchronized by your browser provider according to the provider's account and privacy settings. Installed Marketplace snippets remain in local extension storage.

Navify does not read or store listening history, account details, playlists, passwords, payment information, or audio. It does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, or remote executable code.

The Marketplace downloads its catalog and preview images from the Navify Marketplace repository on GitHub. Installing an item stores its CSS locally and applies it to Spotify Web.

Permissions

storageSaves Navify preferences and installed Marketplace CSS.
open.spotify.comInjects the Navify interface and applies selected customizations only on Spotify Web.
raw.githubusercontent.comDownloads selected theme CSS and preview images from their original Navify and Spicetify repositories. The Marketplace catalog itself is bundled with the extension.

Third-party services

Spotify Web and GitHub are independent services with their own privacy policies. Navify is not affiliated with Spotify. Links opened from the extension are handled by your browser and the destination service.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when extension behavior or permissions change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the Navify browser repository.
